Master's thesis, muEDM experiment

May 2024 – May 2025

muEDM Group · Laboratory for Particle Physics, Paul Scherrer Institute, Switzerland · Supervisor: Dr. Philipp Schmidt-Wellenburg

Characterised the 6D phase space of the µE1 muon beamline in its Z-configuration for muEDM Phase II. Prepared the June 2024 beamtime, including detector holders designed in Onshape, and took part in data acquisition. Analysed rates, emittances, momentum spread and dispersion, and modelled the beam in TRANSPORT, XSuite and G4beamline (agreement with data within 0.21 % for XSuite). The 125 MeV/c tune identified (1.1 × 10⁸ μ⁺/s; 920 / 213 mm·mrad) is the Phase II beam quoted in EPJ C 85, 622. Case study

Beam dynamics · C++/ROOT · Python · XSuite · TRANSPORT · G4beamline · Onshape
