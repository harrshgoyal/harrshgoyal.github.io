What I work on

muEDM in brief, for readers outside the field.

A permanent electric dipole moment (EDM) of a fundamental particle would violate time-reversal symmetry, and with it CP symmetry. The Standard Model predicts a muon EDM far too small to measure, so seeing one would point directly to new physics.

muEDM at PSI uses the frozen-spin technique. Muons circulate in a compact solenoid, and a radial electric field is tuned so that the spin stays aligned with the momentum (it is "frozen"). An EDM would then slowly tilt the spin out of the orbital plane. That tilt shows up as a growing up–down asymmetry in the decay positrons.

For my master's thesis I characterised the muon beam that Phase II will use (case study). My PhD covers analysis, systematic studies and hardware for the measurement itself.

Master's thesis · 2024–2025PSI · IISER Pune

6D phase space of the µE1 muon beam for muEDM Phase II

I measured and modelled the muon beam that PSI's µE1 beamline delivers in its Z-configuration, which had not been characterised before.

Question
Which µE1 beam tune gives muEDM Phase II the most usable 125 MeV/c muons, and can simulations reproduce it?
My role
June 2024 beamtime (setup, detector holders I designed in Onshape, data taking), the full analysis, and modelling in TRANSPORT, XSuite and G4beamline.
Result
The Z-configuration's 125 MeV/c tune gave the highest rate measured, 1.1 × 10⁸ μ⁺/s, with emittances of 920 / 213 mm·mrad. These emittances are the Phase II beam parameters in the 2025 design paper. XSuite reproduced the measured beam to within 0.21 %.

Publication · 2025muEDM collaboration

A compact frozen-spin trap for the muon EDM

The collaboration's design paper for muEDM, published in Eur. Phys. J. C. It sets out the physics case, the frozen-spin method and the Phase I apparatus at PSI.

Key numbers
Phase I: 23 MeV/c muons in a 2.5 T solenoid, with an effective field of about 165 MV/m in the muon rest frame. Target sensitivity σ(d) ≤ 4 × 10⁻²¹ e·cm in 200 days. Phase II: 125 MeV/c muons, aiming for 6 × 10⁻²³ e·cm.
My part
Co-author. The Phase II beam it specifies (over 10⁸ μ⁺/s at 125 MeV/c, with emittances of 920 and 213 π mm·mrad) matches my µE1 measurements.

  • Experiment design
