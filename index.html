ABOUT HARRSH

01

Physics at
small scales.

I completed an Integrated BS–MS in Scientific Research, with an MS in Physics, at IISER Pune in 2025. My research has taken me from cosmology and gravitational lensing to accelerator and particle physics.

My recent work includes a master’s thesis on characterising the six-dimensional phase space of a muon beam for the muon electric dipole moment experiment at PSI, and software for analysing higher-order cosmological clustering.
